Clinical Director

Dr. Ashwini K.S, MDS

Expert in Microscope Root Canal Treatments & Aesthetic Smile Designing

Dr. Ashwini K.S obtained her BDS and specialized Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics. She has dedicated her career to micro-endodontics, aesthetic restorations, and pain-free treatments.

As an Endodontist, her primary clinical focus is saving compromised teeth through advanced root canal treatment under dental microscopes. Microscope dentistry ensures high magnification and superior illumination, making root canals extremely precise and painless. She is also a recognized smile designer, crafting custom dental veneers and laminates that restore confidence and bite dynamics.

She has served in academic and clinical institutions (including SJM Dental College and Hospital), sharing her dental expertise and mentoring dental students. Today, she leads the clinical operations at Dr. Ashwini's Dental Clinic, ensuring high-quality, international-grade sterilization and patient-focused solutions.

Clinical Standards

Why Choose Us?

We follow strict clinical guidelines to deliver safe and high-precision treatments to all patients.

Strict Sterilization

We prioritize safety by running multi-stage autoclave sterilization cycles for all clinical instruments and using disposable kits for every consultation.

Our treatment pathways are customized to deliver a relaxed atmosphere. We combine pain-free anesthetic technologies with gentle bedside care.
